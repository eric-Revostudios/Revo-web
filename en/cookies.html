1. What are cookies?

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser when you visit it. They are used to make the site work, remember your preferences, measure its use and, when you allow it, show you relevant advertising. Alongside cookies we use similar technologies (pixels, local storage) which we group here under the same term.
